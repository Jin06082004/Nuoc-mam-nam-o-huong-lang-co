# Content Required Before Production

Do not publish invented brand, legal, operational, or product claims. The storefront currently uses only the known store name and keeps other business settings empty until the owner supplies values.

## Owner-provided business information

- Official public domain and canonical URL.
- Official business address and contact phone/email.
- Approved store description and logo.
- Shipping fee, free-shipping threshold, and shipping coverage.
- Official return/refund policy and customer-support process.
- Approved Facebook URL; TikTok and YouTube remain empty until real URLs are supplied.
- Bank display details only if the owner chooses to enable bank transfer.

## Product and editorial content

- Official product names, descriptions, prices, variants, and stock.
- Owner-approved product, category, and hero images with appropriate rights and alt text.
- Published blog articles and images, if the owner intends to use the blog.
- Real YouTube videos hosted on supported YouTube domains, if any.

## Readiness checks

- Verify that every public price, shipping rule, policy, and contact detail is owner-approved.
- Remove test/demo records before release.
- Verify every image/social/video URL and ensure unavailable fields are omitted rather than replaced with placeholders.
- Obtain legal/business review for claims, privacy notices, terms, and return policy.
